# 🌸 "I'm Sorry Prachi" — Interactive Apology Website

A heartfelt, romantic, and playful interactive website created especially for **Prachi**, crafted with love, sincere remorse, and thoughtful touches to bring a smile back to her face.

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **🧸 Interactive Apology Mascot (Teddy Bear)**:
   - Sits with a flower and puppy eyes, blushing and apologetic.
   - Interactive: Tap or click to tickle the bear, making it giggle and share cute thoughts.
   - When forgiven ("Yes" clicked), turns into a super happy bear with heart eyes, waving paws, and celebration confetti!

2. **💌 3D Interactive Apology Letter**:
   - Realistic wax-sealed envelope marked with *"P ❤️"*.
   - Tap the seal to smoothly unfold the letter.
   - Beautifully written sincere apology acknowledging her feelings and promising to do better.
   - Includes a **"Personalize"** button so you can edit the letter with inside jokes, nicknames, or specific memories directly from the browser!

3. **🏃‍♂️ The Unclickable "No" Button (Minigame)**:
   - Asks: *"Will you forgive me, Prachi?"*
   - When she tries to hover or tap **"No 😤"**, the button playfully flees across the screen with funny guilt-trip messages (*"Are you sure? 🥺"*, *"What if I buy you chocolates? 🍫"*, *"I promise 100 hugs! 🧸"*, *"You can't catch me! 💨"*).
   - Each time the "No" button dodges, the **"Yes 🥰"** button grows slightly bigger!

4. **🎉 "Yes" Celebration & Official Decree**:
   - Massive multi-color confetti explosion (`canvas-confetti`).
   - Upbeat celebration fanfare audio.
   - Generates an official **"Certificate of Forgiveness"** signed for Prachi and Harshwardhan.

5. **🎟️ 5 Redeemable Love Coupons ("Peace Offerings")**:
   - ☕ *Favorite Cafe & Boba Date (Any cafe, your treat)*
   - 💆‍♀️ *Long Head & Shoulder Massage on Demand*
   - 🍿 *Movie Night Marathon (She controls the remote 100%)*
   - 🍕 *Midnight Craving Food Delivery (24/7 emergency snack rescue)*
   - 👑 *Legendary "Prachi is 100% Right" Immunity Pass*
   - Each coupon can be clicked to permanently stamp **"REDEEMED 🎉"** with heart particles (saved in `localStorage`).

6. **✨ The "Why You're Irreplaceable" Love Jar**:
   - 20 touching, sweet reminders of why she is your favorite person in the world.
   - Tap to draw notes with chime sound effects.

7. **📸 Polaroid Memory Wall**:
   - Aesthetic polaroid cards with washi tape styling.
   - Built-in photo uploader: easily upload real photos of the two of you directly from your device (saved locally in browser storage!).

8. **🎵 Cozy Lofi Synthesizer Melody**:
   - Ambient, cozy acoustic chord progression built using the Web Audio API.
   - Has a Play/Pause toggle with audio visualizer equalizer bars.
   - Zero external audio files needed — plays smoothly anywhere without broken links!

9. **💖 Touch & Click Sparkles**:
   - Tapping or clicking anywhere on screen generates floating pastel hearts and sparkles.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can open the website immediately in any web browser:

### Option 1: Direct File Open
Double click `index.html` or run:
```bash
open index.html
```

### Option 2: Local HTTP Server (Recommended)
Using Node.js:
```bash
npx serve .
```
Or using Python:
```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```
Then visit `http://localhost:8000` in your browser.

## 🌐 How to Deploy & Send to Prachi Online (100% Free & Takes 1 Minute)

To give Prachi a live link on her phone (WhatsApp / Instagram / iMessage):

### Method A: Netlify Drop (Fastest — No signup needed!)
1. Go to [https://app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop)
2. Drag and drop the `prachi-sorry-website` folder onto the page.
3. Done! Netlify gives you an instant live URL (e.g., `https://for-prachi.netlify.app`) that you can copy and text to her.

### Method B: Vercel CLI
```bash
npx vercel
```
Follow the two prompts, and it will deploy instantly with a live `.vercel.app` URL.

### Method C: GitHub Pages
1. Push this folder to a GitHub repository.
2. Go to **Settings > Pages** and select the `main` branch.
3. Your site will be live at `https://<username>.github.io/<repo-name>`.
